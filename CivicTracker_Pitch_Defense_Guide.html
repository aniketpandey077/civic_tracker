💡 3. Key Innovations

1. Dual-Stage Vision AI

Validates real defects on intake and ensures contractors cannot upload blank or fake photos to close tickets.

2. 15-Day Statutory SLA

Automated countdown timestamps. Overdue tickets escalate publicly to the municipal commissioner.

3. Point-in-Polygon Ward Routing

Spatial ray-casting routes issues to the exact ward (PWD, Sanitation, Water Works) without manual delays.

4. 3-Month Civic Honours Cycle

Permanent Civic Sense Rating (100–1000) with quarterly leaderboard resets awarding printable Govt Certificates.

❓ 4. Tough Judge Questions & Winning Answers
Q1: How do you prevent spam or fake complaint submissions?
"Our client-side compressor optimizes images, and Gemini Vision immediately evaluates the photo. If it detects a non-defect image (like a meme or selfie), the system rejects it before docket creation."
Q2: What prevents contractors from closing tickets with fake 'resolved' photos?
"Our Stage-2 AI Resolution Auditor compares the original defect against the resolution photo. If the pothole or garbage dump is still present, Gemini flags it as NO (Rework Required) and blocks closure."
Q3: Why reset points every 3 months?
"Resetting competition points quarterly ensures newcomers always have a fair chance to win government certificates, while their lifetime Civic Sense Rating is permanently preserved."
Q4: How does the system handle poor internet connectivity?
"Images are compressed down to ~150KB on the browser canvas before transmission, allowing instant uploads even on slow 2G/3G networks."
